I am an Associate Professor in Criminology in the Netherlands.

My research focusses on safety, violence and social relations in prisons, with particular attention to the experiences of incarcerated individuals and staff.

I use qualitative and quantitative methods, including interviews and ethnographic approaches, and multilevel survey analysis. 
My work engages with criminological, sociological and feminist perspectives.

## Research interests

- Violence and safety in prisons
- Gender (norms) and imprisonment
- Prison climate
- Prison staff, occupational/relational safety and institutional security

## Current research

My current research includes the following projects:

### The social ecology of prison violence

A five year project (2023-2027) funded by the Dutch Council for Scientific Research (NWO). 
The project investigates the situational contributors to violence in prison. 
It includes a PhD project (by Dante Hoek, MSc) in which virtual reality is used to study the effect of provocation and bystanders on violent intention.
It includes a postdoctoral project (by Rozalie Lekkerkerk, PhD) in which staff responses to violence are studied.

### The Life in Custody Study

An ongoing project (2016 - current) that includes periodic surveys among all incarcerated individuals in the Netherlands.
The surveys measure prison climate and associated concepts, such as wellbeing and victimisation.
It includes multiple PhD projects that I have supervised:
- Professional re-integration support during incarceration (completed; Amanda Pasma, PhD)
- Reward systems in prison (completed; Jan Maarten Elbers, PhD)
- Safety experiences of incarcerated individuals (ongoing; Sophie Martens, MPhil)

### A gender-binary prison system

A four-year project (2025-2029) on safety, gender and sexuality in prisons. 
The project includes a PhD project (by Yara Abbing, MA).
