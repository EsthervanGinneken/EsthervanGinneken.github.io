My research examines violence, safety and social relations in prison environments.

## The social ecology of violence in prisons

This research examines why violent incidents occur in prisons.
The project focuses on situational factors contributing to prison violence.
It adopts a multi-actor perspective by including perspectives from staff and incarcerated individuals.
The project consists of various sub-projects:

1. Interviews with prison senior management on problems of safety and violence
2. Interviews with incarcerated men about violence
3. Ethnographic research - focused primarily on staff responses to violence
4. A virtual reality study on provocation, bystander behaviour and violent intent
5. Survey research on safety and violence among incarcerated individuals

This research is funded by the Dutch Research Council: project number VI.vidi.211.003.
The project formally runs from 2023 to 2027.

## Safety in women's prisons

This project investigates safety, social dynamics, gender, staff-prisoner relationships and boundary violations in women's prisons in the Netherlands.
It consists of observations, informal conversations and interviews (N = 145) with incarcerated women and staff in all three prisons for women in the Netherlands.
The research was conducted in 2024 and funded by the Dutch Prison Service.

## Prison climate

The Life in Custody Study conducts periodic survey research on prison climate in the Netherlands. 

## Research methods

I am proficient in quantitative and qualitative methods. 
As part of my quantitative work, I often use multilevel analysis, given the nested structure of incarcerated individuals in units and prisons.
For analysis I use SPSS, Stata and (more recently) R.

For my qualitative work, I have used ethnographic methods, conducted (semi-structured) interviews and carried out document analysis.

I have also conducted and published systematic literature reviews.
